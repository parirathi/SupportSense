    Transparent Urgency Rule Specifications:

    • Tier 1 (Critical): Outages, data breach, chargeback, lawsuits, fraud trigger Urgent regardless of sentiment.
    • Tier 2 (Escalation): Terms like urgent, asap, escalate, manager trigger Urgent when sentiment is negative or neutral.
    • Tier 3 (Churn / Dissatisfaction): Cancellation threats or demanding refunds combined with negative sentiment trigger Urgent.
    • Note: Urgency is explicitly rule-driven for human auditability and is not an uninterpretable black-box model.

    Dataset Limitations & Scope:

    • Demonstration benchmark with 150 customer support records (80% train, 20% test).
    • Trained on technical, billing, and software support queries. Out-of-domain conversational text may have degraded accuracy.
    • Sarcasm and subtle rhetorical phrasing may require human operator discretion.
